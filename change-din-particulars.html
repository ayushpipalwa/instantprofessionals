DIN UPDATE PREPARATION

Prepare the changed details and evidence

An initial summary helps us identify the correct update process. The final document list depends on the field, DIN status and current portal requirements.

  • Existing DIN/DPIN, current status and relevant company or LLP associations.
  • Each field to change, its existing value, proposed value and date of change.
  • Identity or address proof supporting the new particulars, with any mismatch explained.
  • Current KYC position, registered contact access and DSC validity where relevant.
  • Previous applications, SRNs, rejection or resubmission messages and any stated deadline.

Share an initial summary through the enquiry. Send identity documents through the agreed secure document-sharing route; do not include passwords, DSC PINs or OTPs.

Official portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Check the current DIN-related forms, instruction kit and portal requirements for the changed field before submission.
CHOOSE THE RIGHT SCOPE

Confirm the update route before filing

MCA provides DIR-6 for changes in director or designated-partner particulars. We check the specific field and current KYC process before deciding which route applies; do not assume every change uses the same form.

This engagement concerns an existing DIN record. For a different requirement, see DIR-3 KYC support, DIN application support or DIN surrender eligibility review.

Focused covers the listed DIN-change work. Extended adds the listed individual Class 3 digital signature application with two-year validity. Confirm any existing DSC, document mismatches, prior failed applications and extra work before selecting a package. A DSC application is not a guarantee of certificate issuance or MCA approval.

For a separate certificate requirement, see digital signature certificate support. Government fees, taxes and outside charges remain subject to the package exclusions below.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin

What should I send for an initial DIN update review?

Identify the existing DIN, the fields to change, the date of change and any portal message. We confirm which evidence is needed before requesting supporting identity or address records.

Is a DIN particulars update the same as KYC?

The correct route depends on the changed field, DIN status and current MCA requirements. Tell us whether your request concerns periodic KYC, changed particulars or a previous unsuccessful submission so that the scope can be confirmed.

Do I need the Extended package if I already have a DSC?

Tell us about the existing certificate and its validity first. Extended includes the listed Class 3 digital signature application; we confirm whether that additional scope is needed before engagement.

Does the quoted fee guarantee immediate approval?

No. We confirm a working timeline after checking the records and proposed change. MCA processing, clarification requests and third-party certificate checks remain outside professional control. Extra work is confirmed separately.
