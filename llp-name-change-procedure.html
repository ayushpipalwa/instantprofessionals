LLP Name Change Procedure and Documents

An LLP name change involves name reservation, partner approval, MCA filings and updates to the LLP's records. Reserving a name is one step; it does not complete the change.

Updated 15 November 2026.

Check the proposed name and approval route

Review the LLP agreement's name-change procedure and the reason for the change. Prepare the decision or consent required by that procedure; where proceeding by consent of all partners, document that consent. A change directed by an authority requires its supporting order or direction.

Check the proposed name before applying, including potential trademark conflicts. MCA name reservation and trademark clearance serve different purposes. If the business activities are also changing, review that change separately because the Form 5 instructions ask for the relevant Form 3 SRN in that situation.

Documents and details checklist

  • LLPIN, current incorporation details, registered office information and LLP contact details.
  • The existing LLP agreement, the applicable name-change clause and the proposed amendment.
  • Partner consent, minutes or resolution supporting the selected approval route; an authority's direction where applicable.
  • The approved RUN-LLP reservation SRN and the exact reserved name. Check that the reservation remains valid.
  • Valid DSCs and MCA signatory details, plus records needed for the PAN, TAN and any other amendments included in your engagement.

Filing sequence

  1. Confirm the proposed name, agreement procedure and required partner approvals.
  2. Apply to reserve the name through RUN-LLP and review any resubmission or rejection before continuing.
  3. Prepare LLP Form 5, Notice for change of name, using the approved reservation SRN and supporting records.
  4. Coordinate the agreement amendment and linked LLP Form 3. MCA's Form 5 instruction kit requires Form 3 as a linked form for the agreement change; do not treat it as an optional later task.
  5. Complete signatures, uploads and fees, retain acknowledgements and respond to any resubmission requirements.
  6. After approval, obtain and check the revised certificate and update the relevant records using the approved name.

What the published packages include

The Focused package at ₹4,500 lists required drafting, one RUN-LLP name approval application, the revised certificate process, agreement revision and filing, and PAN and TAN amendments. The Extended package at ₹8,500 also lists GST registration and Udyam amendments. Registrar approval is not guaranteed by either package.

Government fees, statutory charges, stamp duty, GST, notary and third-party costs are additional unless expressly included in the written quote. Confirm the treatment of a fresh name application, extra resubmission work or unrelated defaults before engagement. The listed package includes one name approval application, not unlimited attempts.

After approval: avoid inconsistent records

Plan updates to bank records, invoices, contracts, licences, the website and customer or supplier records. These are not all listed package inclusions. Allocate responsibility for each update and keep the approval documents together. If only the Focused package is selected, do not assume GST or Udyam amendments are included.

Common questions

Does name reservation complete an LLP name change?

No. RUN-LLP reserves the proposed name. Form 5, the linked agreement filing and the registrar's processing still need to be completed.

Are all post-approval updates included?

No. The Focused package lists PAN and TAN amendments; Extended also lists GST and Udyam amendments. Bank, contract and other record updates need an agreed scope.

Official sources and next steps

MCA LLP Form 5 instruction kit and MCA RUN-LLP help. Check the current official instructions and your case details before filing or agreeing a scope.
