LLP Name Change Procedure

Changing an LLP name generally involves name availability, partner approval, agreement updates and MCA filing. The appropriate sequence depends on the LLP’s facts and the availability of the proposed name.

What documents are commonly required?

The LLP’s existing particulars, proposed names, partner approvals and updated agreement information are typically reviewed. The final checklist depends on the filing route and MCA requirements.

What should be checked before proceeding?

A name should be considered with the LLP’s existing registrations, contracts, banking records, tax profiles and intellectual-property position so the change is coordinated rather than merely filed.
