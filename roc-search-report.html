SERVICE QUESTIONS

ROC search report questions

Who can use an ROC search report?

Lenders, investors and transaction teams may request a report to review available company records. Tell us who will use it and the decision it supports so the scope is appropriate.

Why does the scope of an ROC search report matter?

A report reflects the records and checks within its agreed scope. It is not a guarantee about every liability or a substitute for all financial, legal and commercial due diligence.

Where do ROC search report records come from?

The report draws on available company records accessed through the MCA portal, within the agreed scope. Record availability and the search date should be stated in the report.

When should an ROC search report be refreshed?

Consider a fresh search when the transaction date or purpose changes, or new filings could affect the decision. Agree the record cut-off date with the person relying on the report.

Official guidance: MCA company records. General service information checked on 28 September 2026. The applicable rules and portal requirements depend on your circumstances and prevail over this overview.

SEARCH GUIDE

ROC Search Report: fees, format and download guidance

Clear answers to common questions before you decide the next step.

What is an ROC search report?

An ROC search report is a structured review of the relevant company information available through MCA records. Its precise scope is agreed before work begins and may include company status, directors, charges and filed documents, subject to the records accessible on the portal.

Can I download an ROC search report myself?

Downloading MCA documents and preparing a written ROC search report are different tasks. Our free editable outline is a preparation aid with placeholders, not a completed company report. A written report requires review of the agreed records, with source references and limitations.
