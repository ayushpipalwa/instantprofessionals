INC-20A PREPARATION

Records to prepare for commencement-of-business filing

Start with the company's incorporation date and share-capital details. Confirm that the declaration applies before choosing a filing package.

  • Certificate of incorporation, CIN, memorandum and subscriber details.
  • Share amounts agreed by each subscriber and evidence of the corresponding payments received by the company.
  • Company bank records supporting those receipts, with any mismatch identified for review.
  • Registered-office verification status and relevant company records.
  • Authorised director and DSC details, any regulatory approval relevant to the business, and previous INC-20A filing or query references.

Share basic company details and dates in the initial enquiry. Send bank statements and signed records through an agreed document-sharing route; do not include bank account numbers, DSC PINs, passwords or OTPs in the enquiry message.

Official guidance: MCA INC-20A instruction kit. Check the current form and instructions on the MCA portal before filing.

Review applicability and records before filing INC-20A

INC-20A is the declaration of commencement of business for companies to which the requirement applies. The MCA instruction kit gives a normal filing period of 180 days from incorporation. Confirm the company's eligibility, applicable dates and any relevant exceptions before relying on that period; the general deadline planner is only arithmetic, not an applicability decision.

We review subscriber-payment evidence and company records, identify missing information, confirm the scope and coordinate preparation and filing. A payment towards professional fees does not replace subscriber-capital payment or other underlying requirements. Preserve the filing acknowledgement and supporting records after submission.

Late, incomplete or previously filed cases

Tell us about any missed date, unpaid subscription, earlier filing or portal query at the start. Additional fees, corrective work or other proceedings require a separate assessment; submitting a form should not be assumed to resolve every past default. Portal processing and the result of any authority review are outside professional control.

Related company support: auditor appointment, company annual filing and digital signature certificates. These are separate engagements unless expressly included.
