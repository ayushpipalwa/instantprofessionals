DIN Application Documents and Process

Start by checking whether you already hold a DIN and whether the appointment is in an existing entity or part of a new incorporation. That determines the filing route and the documents to prepare.

Updated 15 November 2026.

Choose the right application route

DIR-3 is the MCA application for a person without a DIN who is to become a director in an existing company or a designated partner in an existing LLP. For a new incorporation, check the relevant incorporation form and its DIN allotment facility before starting a separate application. Do not apply for a second DIN or duplicate a pending application.

Documents and information to prepare

  • A recent passport-size photograph, full name, father's name, date of birth, nationality, occupation and contact details.
  • PAN for an Indian citizen and the identity attachments required by the form. A foreign citizen must provide a passport; confirm the applicable attestation, legalisation and translation requirements before arranging overseas documents.
  • Current residential address details and supporting address evidence accepted by the current form. Check document age and legibility before submission.
  • The existing company's CIN or LLP's LLPIN, proposed appointment details and the supporting authorisation or resolution applicable to the filing.
  • A valid digital signature certificate (DSC) and the authorised signatory details. Confirm MCA registration and association of the signatures before uploading.

This is a preparation checklist, not a substitute for the form-specific attachment list. Names, dates and identification numbers must match across the application, identity records and DSC. Explain differences before filing rather than uploading inconsistent records.

From document review to allotment

  1. Check for an existing or pending DIN and confirm the appointment route.
  2. Review identity, address and entity records; resolve mismatches and confirm the signing requirements.
  3. Prepare and submit the relevant MCA webform. Retain the service request number (SRN).
  4. Complete the digital signatures, signed-document upload and applicable payment steps within the portal's deadlines.
  5. Monitor the acknowledgement and processing status. Respond to any resubmission request and retain the approval communication when issued.

Fees, DSC and timing

The DIN service packages distinguish the application-only option, priority application work and an option including a DSC. Check the selected package before assuming a certificate is included. The written quote should identify professional fees, government charges, taxes and any separately required work.

Priority preparation does not guarantee MCA allotment within 24 hours. Missing documents, signature issues and resubmissions can affect completion. An allotted DIN also does not by itself complete appointment filings or ongoing DIN KYC requirements; confirm those separately.

Common questions

Can I apply if I already have a DIN?

Do not submit a duplicate application. First check the existing DIN status and identify whether an update, KYC or another filing is needed.

How long can DIN allotment take?

Preparation depends on complete, consistent documents and valid signatures. MCA processing and resubmission requirements vary; priority application work is not a guaranteed allotment deadline.
