Compliance Handover Checklist

A practical brief for finance teams and professional advisers: make the scope, evidence, owners and outstanding questions clear before an assignment changes hands.

Discuss a compliance handover

How to use the handover template

  1. Make one copy per assignment and period. Name the file so the receiving person can distinguish it from earlier drafts.
  2. Agree the work being transferred. Record what is included, what is excluded and who reviews and approves the deliverable.
  3. Link to the evidence. Use the agreed document workspace, identify the relevant version and check that the receiving team has appropriate access.
  4. Review exceptions together. Give each unresolved item an owner and a next action before recording the handover outcome.

Keep the working copy private. The public download is a blank template; it does not ask you to upload client records or passwords.

1. Define the assignment

  • Entity or client reference, service and reporting period.
  • Purpose, intended recipient and agreed scope or exclusions.
  • Preparer, reviewer and client approver.
  • Agreed delivery date and any separately verified statutory deadline.
  • Official source and date last checked where a legal requirement is relevant.

Keep a client delivery date separate from a statutory due date. This template supplies neither deadlines nor form-selection advice.

2. Record what is ready

For each item, note its location, version or date, responsible person, review status and reviewer comments.

  • Engagement scope and exclusions.
  • Source records for the period.
  • Reconciliations and exceptions.
  • Prior submissions and acknowledgements.
  • Draft deliverable and approval evidence.
  • Final deliverable and submission evidence.

Keep confidential records in the agreed secure workspace. Do not put access credentials or client documents in a public copy of this template.

3. Resolve open issues

For each unresolved question, identify the missing information, who can resolve it, the effect on the deliverable and the next review date. Mark assumptions explicitly. Do not treat an unavailable record as a clean result.

Worked example: an incomplete reconciliation handover

Illustrative only: A finance team is handing a monthly reconciliation to its reviewer. The working file is ready, but a supporting statement has not been received.

  • Item and version: the reconciliation for the agreed period, with the draft version identified in the secure workspace.
  • Status: prepared, awaiting supporting evidence; not reviewed or complete.
  • Owner and next action: the assigned finance contact obtains the missing statement and records where it is saved.
  • Reviewer action: compare the statement with the reconciliation, document differences and confirm the revised status.
  • Handover outcome: the receiving team acknowledges the open item and the next review date. An acknowledgement is not approval of the underlying figures.

Use the same pattern for a missing approval, unresolved balance or outstanding acknowledgement. Describe the evidence gap rather than marking the entire assignment complete.

4. Close the handover with evidence

The receiving professional should confirm the scope, records received, unresolved items and next action. Record completion only when the agreed evidence is available. An internal status of complete does not by itself establish statutory compliance or government acceptance.

Prepare a service-specific brief

  • Income tax return filing: agree the taxpayer, period, income categories and reconciliation scope.
  • Company annual filing: agree the entity, financial period, relevant approvals and records to review.
  • Trademark work: agree the applicant, mark representation, intended goods or services, and search or filing scope.

These prompts identify the brief; they do not determine eligibility, forms, deadlines or legal conclusions.

Compliance handover questions

Can I edit the template in Word or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Download the TXT file and copy its headings and fields into your preferred working document. Keep a single agreed version and identify the reviewer; the download is not an automated filing tool.

What if some records are missing?

List each missing record, its owner, the effect on the assignment and the next review date. Do not record a clean review or completed submission when the evidence is unavailable.

Should passwords be included in the handover?

No. Record the system owner and arrange access through the organisation's approved process. Keep credentials, identity records and confidential client documents out of public templates and enquiry messages.

Does the checklist establish statutory compliance?

No. It records coordination and evidence status. Applicable requirements, filings and deadlines need a separate review for the entity, service and period.

Ask about handover scope

A scope discussion starts an enquiry. The free template does not include a paid engagement; any professional work and fees are agreed separately.
