Trademark Watch Service Cost

Trademark watch support helps businesses monitor potentially similar marks published for relevant classes. Cost depends on the number of marks, classes, monitoring frequency and whether an identified matter needs further professional action.

What does the service include?

The agreed scope may include periodic monitoring, a concise alert and an initial practical review. Opposition, evidence, hearings and government fees require a separately confirmed scope.

How should a business decide?

Start with the marks and classes most important to your business. A registered mark does not eliminate the value of monitoring; early awareness can help you assess available options within the applicable time limits.

Need service-specific guidance?

We review the facts, documents and current portal requirements before confirming scope, pricing and timeline.
