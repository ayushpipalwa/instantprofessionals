Trademark Watch Service Cost and Scope

The published Focused package is ₹2,300 for watching a trademark application through its stages. Monitoring other applicants' potentially conflicting marks is a different scope and requires a separate quote.

Updated 15 November 2026.

What does the ₹2,300 package cover?

The Trademark Watch Service package describes its scope as “End to end watch of TM application till last stage”. Treat this as application-status follow-up, not a blanket subscription to search all new trademarks or an engagement to conduct legal proceedings.

Before work begins, the written quote should identify the application number or numbers covered, the starting stage, what counts as the final stage, the checking frequency and how updates will be shared. The published price does not specify a monthly or annual billing period, so do not assume one.

Application follow-up versus conflict monitoring

  • Application follow-up: checking the progress of the identified application and flagging status developments for review. The registry's application-status service is relevant here.
  • Conflict monitoring: searching newly published or other relevant applications for identical or similar marks in agreed classes and territories. This needs its own search criteria, reporting frequency and quotation.

A status update or a possible-conflict alert is information for a decision. It does not itself answer an examination objection, file an opposition, stop another application or secure registration.

What affects a separate monitoring quote?

For broader conflict monitoring, provide the word mark or logo, relevant classes, goods or services, territories and the period you want covered. Agree whether the search includes spelling variations, similar expressions or logo comparisons, and how often results are reviewed. Do not assume these features are included in the ₹2,300 application-watch fee.

Costs and work to confirm separately

The published amount is a professional fee. Government fees, statutory charges, GST, subscriptions and third-party costs are additional unless expressly included. Examination replies, opposition notices, hearings, legal opinions, negotiations and litigation require an agreed separate scope and fee. Monitoring does not include unlimited responses or representation.

Ask for an itemised quote showing the monitoring work, any follow-up advice, external charges and renewal or extension terms where relevant. There is no published fixed fee here for broader conflict monitoring, so a made-up monthly rate or market range would not be a reliable comparison.

How to start and use an update

  1. Share the application details and explain whether you need status follow-up or broader conflict monitoring.
  2. Confirm the scope, frequency, reporting method, end point and exclusions in writing.
  3. Review each update and ask whether a response or separate professional assessment is needed.
  4. If action is required, agree the next engagement promptly. Checking status does not extend a registry deadline.

Common questions

Is conflict monitoring included in the ₹2,300 package?

The published package covers watching a trademark application through its stages. Broader searches for potentially conflicting marks require a separately agreed scope and quote.

Does a watch fee include objections or opposition work?

No. Replies, opposition, hearings and other legal action require a separate agreed scope and fee; an alert does not itself stop another application.
