Overview

LLPs must file annual returns and statements of accounts to remain compliant. We assist with Form 11 and Form 8 preparation, review, and e‑filing.

Documents Required

  • Statement of accounts (Balance Sheet, P&L)
  • Details of partners and capital contribution
  • Previous year’s filed forms (for reference)

Process

  1. Preparation of Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency)
  2. Preparation of Form 11 (Annual Return)
  3. Review and approval by designated partners
  4. E‑filing with MCA and intimation of SRN/ARN

Fees & Timeline

Fees depend on the LLP’s capital and turnover. Filings are typically completed within 2–5 working days from receipt of complete information.
