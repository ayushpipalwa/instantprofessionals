DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Records to prepare for an IEC application

First identify the applicant entity and whether it already holds an IEC. An existing profile may need linking or modification rather than a new application.

  • Active entity PAN, matching legal name and date of birth or incorporation, plus constitution records where applicable.
  • Business address evidence; where the proof is not in the applicant firm's name, prepare the premises owner's NOC with the address proof.
  • Active bank account in the firm's name and a cancelled cheque or bank certificate.
  • Authorised-person details and signing arrangements using the applicable DSC or Aadhaar e-sign route.
  • Existing IEC, application references, portal queries and any differences between business, bank and PAN records.

The official DGFT IEC manual explains application documents and validation. Check the latest instructions under Learn > Online Help & FAQs on the DGFT portal before filing. Bank and portal validation can affect completion; issuance is not guaranteed within a fixed period.

Agree secure document sharing after the initial enquiry. Do not enter PAN, Aadhaar, bank-account numbers, passwords or one-time codes in the enquiry message.

Authoritative portal: DGFT. The portal and applicable law prevail over general website information.
