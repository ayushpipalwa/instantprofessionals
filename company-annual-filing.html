SERVICE QUESTIONS

Company annual-filing questions

What does company annual filing include?

Company annual filing covers applicable financial-statement and annual-return submissions to the Registrar of Companies. The AOC-4 and MGT-7 form families serve different purposes; the correct variants depend on the company.

Who is company annual-filing support for?

This service is for companies seeking help reviewing and preparing their annual filings. LLPs follow a separate process; see our LLP annual-filing service for that requirement.

Why review the accounts and company records together?

Financial statements and annual returns draw on different records. Reviewing them together helps identify inconsistencies in company details, shareholding and other information before the relevant forms are prepared.

Where are company annual filings submitted?

Applicable forms are submitted through the official MCA portal. Professional support assists preparation and filing; it does not replace the company’s approvals or the authority’s processing.

When should a company prepare its annual filings?

Plan the work alongside accounts finalisation and the applicable meeting and filing calendar. Confirm the company category, financial year, relevant meeting dates and current extensions before setting filing deadlines.

How should a company prepare its annual-filing records?

Collect financial statements, company and ownership records, relevant meeting documents and previous filing details. Identify pending filings or changed company particulars early so the scope and required approvals can be reviewed.

Does annual ROC filing cover every company change?

Annual filing and event-based changes have different scopes. Tell us about changes to the company as well as the year being filed so we can identify any separate work. For a name change, see company name change support. LLPs should review LLP annual filing instead.

Can you review more than one pending filing year?

Share the pending years, available accounts and previous filing acknowledgements. We review the records before quoting for each year and identifying any additional work or statutory charges. A published single-year package should not be treated as a quote for clearing every historical default.

Official guidance: Ministry of Corporate Affairs. General service information checked on 28 September 2026. The applicable rules and portal requirements depend on your circumstances and prevail over this overview.
