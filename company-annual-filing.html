Overview

Every company must comply with annual filing requirements under the Companies Act, including financial statements and annual returns. We handle end‑to‑end compliance to avoid penalties and strike‑off risks.

Documents Required

  • Financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, etc.)
  • Board and shareholders’ meeting details
  • Register of members and charges (if applicable)
  • Previous year’s filed forms (for reference)

Process

  1. Review of financials and compliance status
  2. Preparation of MGT‑7, AOC‑4 and related forms
  3. Review and approval by directors
  4. E‑filing with MCA and intimation of SRN/ARN

Fees & Timeline

Fees depend on the company’s share capital and turnover. Filings are typically completed within 3–7 working days from receipt of complete information.
