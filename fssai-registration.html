SERVICE QUESTIONS

FSSAI registration and food licence questions

What is the difference between FSSAI registration and a food licence?

Registration, State Licence and Central Licence are different application routes for food businesses. The appropriate route depends on the applicable eligibility criteria, rather than the business name alone.

Who is FSSAI application support for?

Food-business operators seeking help with a new application or an existing registration or licence can use this service. Describe your products, activities and premises so the requirement can be assessed.

Why review the food activity and premises before applying?

FoSCoS eligibility and documents depend on the business category and relevant operating details. Reviewing activities, premises, turnover and capacity helps avoid selecting a route that does not fit the business.

Where can I apply for FSSAI registration or a licence?

Use the official FoSCoS portal for applications and related services. Instant Professionals offers preparation and coordination support; the competent authority processes and decides the application.

When should I review my FSSAI registration or licence?

Review the requirement while planning a food business and when relevant operating details change. For an existing registration or licence, check its status, applicable fee payments and modification requirements on FoSCoS. FSSAI introduced perpetual validity in 2026; routine renewal is no longer required under that framework.

How do I prepare for an FSSAI application?

Start with the consultation checklist above, then check FoSCoS eligibility and the documents for the selected activity and category. Prepare the application and respond to any authority queries; approval and timing are not guaranteed.

Can a home kitchen, restaurant or food seller use the same checklist?
