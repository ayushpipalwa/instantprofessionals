FSSAI registration, licence and FoSCoS support

Food-business requirements depend on the nature of activity, turnover, capacity, premises and other eligibility criteria. We review your facts before recommending Basic Registration, State Licence or Central Licence.

Review business activity and available information to identify the applicable registration or licence route.

FoSCoS coordination

Application and follow-up support through the official Food Safety Compliance System, subject to authority processing.
