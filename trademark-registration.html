Overview

Trademark registration protects your brand name, logo, or tagline. We handle class selection, application drafting, filing, and objection/opposition replies.

Documents Required

  • Applicant name and type (individual, startup, company, etc.)
  • Logo / wordmark (if applicable)
  • List of goods / services and relevant class(es)
  • Identity and address proof of the applicant
  • MSME / Startup certificate (if claiming concession)

Process

  1. Trademark search and class identification
  2. Filing of TM‑A application
  3. Examination and issuance of examination report (if any)
  4. Response to objections / hearing (if required)
  5. Advertisement and registration certificate

Fees & Timeline

Government fees and professional charges vary by applicant type and number of classes. Typical timeline is 8–18 months for registration, subject to objections and oppositions.
