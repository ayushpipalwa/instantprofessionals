WHO IT SUITS

Who a private limited company suits

A private company is a separate legal entity under the Companies Act, 2013. It is usually the right vehicle when two or more promoters want limited liability and a share structure.

Two or more promoters

You need at least two subscribers and two directors. The same people can hold both roles. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital.

Equity and limited liability

The company owns its assets and the members’ liability is limited to the unpaid amount on their shares. This is the usual structure when investment is planned.

Not a sole founder

A single Indian citizen who does not want a co-founder should look at OPC registration. Professional partnerships often fit an LLP better.

DOCUMENTS

Documents required

The final list depends on whether the promoters are Indian residents, NRIs or foreign nationals, and on the registered-office arrangement. Names and identity numbers must match the DSC.

  • PAN, photograph and address proof of every subscriber and director. A foreign national uses a passport, and the DSC must match that name.
  • Proposed names in order of preference, a short description of the main objects, authorised and paid-up capital, and the shareholding.
  • Digital signature certificates for the subscribers and directors. A DIN is not a precondition: SPICe+ can allot a DIN for up to three proposed directors who do not already have one.
  • Registered-office proof: an ownership document, or a rent or lease agreement with a no-objection letter from the owner, plus a recent utility bill.
  • Director consent (DIR-2) and the subscribers’ declaration (INC-9). We prepare these with the SPICe+ set; they are not extra forms you draft alone.

Authoritative portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Portal instructions prevail over this overview.

PROCESS

Step-by-step process

  1. Confirm the structure. Check that a private company, rather than an OPC, LLP or proprietorship, matches the promoters and the proposed activity.
  2. Reserve the name. Apply through SPICe+ Part A, or through RUN where a separate reservation is needed. Use the approved name in Part B while the reservation is valid, and confirm the current validity on the MCA portal.
  3. Prepare SPICe+ Part B. Complete INC-32 with capital, subscribers, directors and the registered office. Link e-MOA (INC-33) and e-AOA (INC-34).
  4. File AGILE-PRO-S where it is useful. The linked AGILE-PRO-S form can cover GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, Profession Tax in Maharashtra, bank-account opening and Shops and Establishment registration. Each registration still follows its own approval.
  5. Sign and track. Subscribers and directors sign with their DSC. Keep the SRN, answer any resubmission, and collect the certificate of incorporation, PAN and TAN.
  6. Plan the first filings. A company with share capital files the commencement declaration (INC-20A) within 180 days of incorporation, after the subscribers have paid for their shares. Plus and Complete include that filing. Focused does not.
TIMELINE

Indicative timeline

These are working estimates after a complete document set. MCA processing, resubmission and DSC verification are outside a fixed promise.

  • DSC: commonly one or two working days after the identity check is complete.
  • Name reservation: often a few working days when the name is available and MCA does not ask for a resubmission.
  • Certificate of incorporation: often about 7 to 15 working days after a complete SPICe+ filing. Resubmission resets that expectation.
  • INC-20A: within 180 days of incorporation for a company with share capital, and only after share money is received.

FAQ

Common questions

What is SPICe+?

SPICe+ (INC-32) is the MCA integrated web form for incorporating a company. Part A reserves the name. Part B is the incorporation application and is linked to the e-MOA, e-AOA and, where used, AGILE-PRO-S.

How many directors and shareholders are required?

A private company needs at least two members and at least two directors. The same two people can be both. At least one director must stay in India for 182 days or more in the financial year, applied proportionately in the year of incorporation, under section 149 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Can an NRI or a foreign national be a director or shareholder?

Yes, subject to the resident-director rule and to FEMA reporting where the investment is from outside India. A foreign national needs a passport and a DSC in that name. We confirm the route from the promoters’ residence and the source of capital before filing.

Do we need a DIN and DSC before filing?

Every subscriber and director who signs needs a DSC. A DIN is allotted through SPICe+ for up to three proposed directors who do not already have one. A person who already has a DIN must use that DIN and must not apply again.

What does AGILE-PRO-S cover?

It is the linked form for GSTIN, EPFO, ESIC, Profession Tax in Maharashtra, opening a bank account and Shops and Establishment registration, as those regimes apply. Filing the form is not the same as approval of each registration.
