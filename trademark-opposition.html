Overview

When a trademark is advertised in the journal, any third party may file an opposition. We represent clients in opposition proceedings, both as opponent and as applicant.

Documents Required

  • Trademark application / registration details
  • Grounds of opposition or counter‑statement
  • Evidence of use and reputation (affidavits, invoices, advertisements, etc.)

Process

  1. Filing of notice of opposition or counter‑statement
  2. Exchange of evidence (affidavits and documents)
  3. Hearing before the Registrar
  4. Final order and further appeals (if any)

Fees & Timeline

Fees depend on the stage and complexity of the matter. Opposition proceedings typically span 12–24 months.
