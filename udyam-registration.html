PREPARATION

Information to prepare for Udyam registration

The official process is online, paperless and based on self-declaration. No documents or proof need to be uploaded for registration; supporting records help you check that the information is accurate.

  • Applicant constitution and the appropriate authorised person's Aadhaar details.
  • Enterprise PAN and GSTIN where applicable, with consistent legal-name information.
  • Business address, bank details and manufacturing or service activities for the relevant NIC codes.
  • Investment and turnover information to check against linked Income Tax and GST records.
  • Any existing Udyam or UAM number, to identify an update or migration instead of a duplicate registration.

Use the official Udyam portal for registration and current instructions. Do not put Aadhaar, PAN, bank numbers, passwords or OTPs in the enquiry message.

MSME classification and registration scope

The official portal lists the following investment and annual-turnover ceilings, effective from 1 April 2025. Both limits apply to the relevant category.

  • Micro: investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore.
  • Small: investment up to ₹25 crore and turnover up to ₹100 crore.
  • Medium: investment up to ₹125 crore and turnover up to ₹500 crore.

An enterprise should not file more than one Udyam registration; manufacturing and service activities can be included in the same registration. Registration does not require renewal. Existing UAM holders should use the migration route shown on the official portal.

Udyam is not a substitute for other applicable registrations. Review GST registration separately and consider IEC requirements for import or export activities.

Source: Ministry of MSME Udyam portal. Guidance checked on 22 November 2026.
