Overview

When the Trademark Registry issues an examination report with objections, a detailed written reply must be filed within the prescribed time. We draft and file comprehensive responses to overcome objections.

Documents Required

  • Examination report / objection letter
  • Details of use of the mark (affidavit of use, invoices, etc., if applicable)
  • Any supporting case laws or precedents (if available)

Process

  1. Analysis of objections and mark distinctiveness
  2. Drafting of reply with legal arguments and evidence
  3. Filing of reply and tracking status
  4. Preparation for hearing (if required)

Fees & Timeline

Fees depend on the complexity and number of objections. Replies are typically filed within 2–5 working days from engagement.
