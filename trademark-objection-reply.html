REPLY PREPARATION

Prepare the examination report and application records

Share the complete report and its receipt details first so the response period and each objection can be reviewed against the application.

  • Trademark application number, class, applicant details and the application as filed.
  • Complete examination report, receipt or communication date, current status and any hearing notice.
  • Mark representation, goods or services specification and cited-mark details from the report.
  • Adoption history and dated evidence supporting the actual use claimed, where relevant.
  • Earlier replies, authorisation records and the original filing-provider details.

Provide accurate records through the agreed document-sharing route. Do not include portal passwords, OTPs or DSC PINs in an enquiry.

Official source: IP India — Trade Marks Rules, 2017. Rule 33 addresses examination reports, responses and hearings. Confirm the report, receipt date and current requirements promptly.

Understand the objection before preparing a reply

An examination objection is raised by the Trade Marks Registry during application review. It is different from a third-party opposition after publication. The report and application file determine the response; a standard template cannot establish that an objection has been answered.

We review the stated grounds, cited marks, specification and available evidence before confirming the drafting and filing scope. Claims about adoption, ownership or use must be supported by the applicant's actual records. Do not create or backdate evidence to support a reply.

Check the response period immediately

Rule 33(4) provides that failure to respond within one month from receipt of the examination report may lead the Registrar to treat the application as abandoned. This is not a generic 30-day calculator: the actual communication and receipt records need review. If the period may have expired or a hearing is already listed, seek a case-specific assessment without assuming a late reply will be accepted.

The ₹9,600 Complete package combines the listed Udyam registration assistance, one trademark application in one class for a small enterprise, and the listed examination-report reply. It is not an unlimited-response or guaranteed-registration package. Confirm the applicant category, number of classes, objections and any earlier filing before selecting it.

Hearing representation, opposition proceedings, appeals, additional classes or further replies require a separately confirmed scope unless expressly included in the written quote. Government fees, taxes and third-party costs remain additional under the pricing note.

Track the outcome after filing

Retain the submitted response and filing acknowledgement, then monitor official communications. A reply acknowledgement is not acceptance or registration. Further examination or a hearing may follow; no favourable outcome or fixed processing time is guaranteed.
