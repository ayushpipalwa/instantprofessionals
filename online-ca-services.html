FEES AND SCOPE

How much do online CA services cost?

The fee depends on the service, filing period and records involved. Compare the published package for the work you need; there is no single price covering every online CA or compliance requirement.

  • Income-tax return filing: compare the ITR filing fees and inclusions for a salaried individual, a non-audit taxpayer or an audit taxpayer. The audit-taxpayer filing package excludes audit work and the audit report.
  • GST returns: check the GST return packages against your GSTIN, return type, filing frequency and entry volume. A single NIL return is a different scope from an annual package; it is not a price for all GST compliance.
  • Bookkeeping: compare the monthly accounting packages by entry limit. Identify the number of months and any backlog before choosing a package.

The written quote confirms the applicable scope. Government fees, statutory charges, stamp duty, GST, certificates, subscriptions and third-party costs are additional unless a package expressly includes them. Ask whether reconciliation, corrections, notices, audit or certification are included before proceeding; they should not be assumed to be part of a filing fee.

For example, a business needing bookkeeping and GST returns should compare both scopes rather than treating one return-filing package as a combined accounting engagement. Share the relevant periods and approximate transaction volume so the requirements can be assessed together.

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

What to prepare for an online consultation

Start with a short description of the requirement. The exact document checklist depends on the service and is confirmed after the initial review.

  • For an individual: note the filing year, broad income sources, whether a return has already been filed, and any deadline or notice you want help with.
  • For a business: describe the entity type, services needed, relevant months or years, approximate entry volume, and whether accounting records are current or need catch-up work.
  • For an existing compliance matter: identify the filing or registration involved, the current status and the outcome you need, such as a fresh filing, correction or response.

Have relevant income statements, transaction records, previous acknowledgements or notices available for the agreed review. You do not need to put sensitive documents, portal passwords or OTPs in an initial enquiry. Confirm the document-sharing and verification steps with the professional handling the work.

ENGAGEMENT PROCESS

From first enquiry to agreed deliverables

  1. Describe the requirement. Use the enquiry form to explain the service, period and deadline; an enquiry starts a discussion and does not itself complete a filing.
  2. Confirm scope and professional involvement. Clarify the appropriate professional, package inclusions, exclusions, charges and expected timeline before engagement.
  3. Prepare records and resolve questions. Follow the service-specific checklist and identify missing information before preparation proceeds.
  4. Review and complete the agreed work. Confirm the information and any required authorisation or verification. For filing work, retain the applicable acknowledgement and clarify any remaining action.

Timing depends on complete records, the agreed scope and any portal or authority processing. Online coordination does not remove a required verification, physical-presence or statutory certification step.
