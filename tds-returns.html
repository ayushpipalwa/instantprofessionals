TDS STATEMENT PREPARATION

Records to prepare for a TDS return

Identify the deductor, filing period and payment categories before requesting a quote. The records below help scope regular quarterly statement work; the applicable form and utility depend on the period and transaction.

  • Deductor name, TAN, contact and authorised-signatory details.
  • Relevant financial year or tax year, quarter, payment categories and previous filing acknowledgements.
  • Deductee names and PANs, payment amounts and dates, and deduction workings.
  • Tax-deposit challans and allocation workings to reconcile payments with deductee entries.
  • Salary records where relevant, applicable certificates or supporting declarations, and any unresolved errors or notices.

Flag missing PANs, unmatched challans and earlier statements that need correction. Use our compliance handover checklist to assign record owners, or discuss bookkeeping support if the underlying records need preparation.

Preparation guidance checked on 16 November 2026. See the Income Tax Department's TDS compliance guidance. This is an engagement checklist, not a universal statutory document list; period-specific forms and portal requirements must be confirmed.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin

What determines the final scope?

The applicant type, facts, available records, pending defaults, notices and the required filing or deliverable determine the final scope.

How long will the process take?

We confirm an expected turnaround after document review. Government or portal processing time remains outside professional control.

Are government charges included?

Only when expressly stated in the written quotation. Government fees, taxes, stamp duty and third-party charges are otherwise additional.

From records to filing acknowledgement

  1. Confirm the deductor, periods, payment categories, entry volumes and service scope.
  2. Reconcile challans and deductee data, identify exceptions and resolve missing records.
  3. Prepare and validate the applicable statement using the current period-specific utility, then obtain approval of the figures.
  4. File through the applicable route and retain the acknowledgement; review processing results and any further work required.

A successful upload does not by itself resolve every mismatch or notice. Tax deposits, statement filing and certificate work are distinct steps; confirm the work covered by your package.

Original filing, correction or a different TDS route?

If a statement has already been filed and needs changes, review TDS return correction and revision support before purchasing original-filing work. Share the earlier acknowledgement and the issue to be reviewed so any additional scope can be quoted.

Some transactions use a separate challan-cum-statement route rather than a regular quarterly statement. Describe the payment and filing period first; do not assume every TDS transaction fits these packages. For a broader tax filing requirement, see income-tax return filing.
