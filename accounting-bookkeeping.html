When this service fits

Books that support compliance and management.

Common questions

Before onboarding.

Can you clean up earlier periods?

Yes, after a diagnostic review. Backlog work is scoped separately because record gaps and reconciliation issues are not predictable from transaction counts alone.

Is accounting software included?

Software subscriptions, migration and configuration are included only when stated. We first confirm the existing platform and access model.

Are tax filings included?

Only the filings expressly listed in the written scope. Reliable books support filings, but the filing service, review and responsibility are separate deliverables.

How often will reports be issued?

Monthly, quarterly or another agreed frequency, depending on the close cycle and information availability.
