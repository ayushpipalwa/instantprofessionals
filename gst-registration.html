Overview

GST registration is mandatory for businesses exceeding the prescribed turnover limit and for specific categories of suppliers. We assist with end‑to‑end registration, including document preparation, form filing, and ARN tracking.

Documents Required

  • PAN of the applicant / business
  • Aadhaar of the promoter / authorized signatory
  • Proof of business registration (if applicable)
  • Address proof of principal place of business
  • Bank account details / cancelled cheque
  • Letter of authorization / board resolution (if applicable)

Process

  1. Submission of details and documents
  2. Preparation and filing of GST REG‑01
  3. Clarification / additional information (if any)
  4. Approval and issuance of GST registration certificate

Fees & Timeline

Professional fees depend on the nature of the entity and number of states. Timelines typically range from 3–10 working days, subject to department processing and clarifications.
