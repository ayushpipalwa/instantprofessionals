SERVICE QUESTIONS

GST registration questions

What is GST registration?

GST registration identifies a business as a registered taxpayer under the GST system. A successful application results in a GST identification number, commonly called a GSTIN.

Who is GST registration support for?

This service helps businesses assess their registration position and prepare an application. Share your activity, turnover, operating states and business structure so the applicable route can be reviewed.

Why review eligibility before applying for GST registration?

The registration route and information needed depend on the business. An initial review helps identify the appropriate applicant, business premises and authorised signatory before documents are uploaded.

Where can I apply for GST registration?

Applications are submitted through the official GST Portal. Instant Professionals provides application support; the GST authorities process the application and decide the outcome.

When should a business review its GST registration requirement?

Review the requirement when starting a business or changing its activity, turnover or operating locations. Confirm the applicable registration trigger and filing timeline before relying on a general turnover figure.

How does the GST registration process work?

Start with an eligibility and document review, prepare the portal application, complete the applicable verification steps and track its status. After registration, review the applicable GST return-filing requirements.

Do I need a new GST registration or an amendment?

Share your existing GSTIN and the details that have changed before starting another application. Our GST registration amendment service helps review changes to an existing registration; we confirm the appropriate route after checking the facts.

Does the registration fee include GST return filing?

The Focused package covers registration, while the Plus and Complete packages include return filing within their stated periods and entry limits. Compare the package scope above and request a written quote covering application support and any additional charges. For recurring compliance, see GST return filing and reconciliation.
