Overview

Director Identification Number (DIN) is mandatory for all company directors. We assist with new DIN applications and DIN‑related compliance for individuals and entities.

Documents Required

  • PAN (mandatory for Indian nationals)
  • Passport (for foreign nationals)
  • Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, driving license, voter ID, etc.)
  • Photograph and specimen signature

Process

  1. Collection and verification of documents
  2. Filing of DIR‑3 / SPICe+ (as applicable)
  3. Allocation of DIN by MCA
  4. Intimation of DIN and related credentials

Fees & Timeline

Fees vary by applicant type and mode of application. DIN is typically allotted within 2–7 working days from submission.
