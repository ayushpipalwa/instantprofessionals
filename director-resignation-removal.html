CESSATION PREPARATION

Records to prepare for a director cessation

Identify whether the request concerns a resignation or a proposed removal before choosing a package. The evidence and company process need separate review.

  • Company CIN, constitutional documents and current director details.
  • Director DIN, designation, appointment records and relevant signing arrangements.
  • For resignation: the notice, evidence of delivery to the company and the dates stated in the records.
  • For proposed removal: available notices, meeting records, resolutions and correspondence for review.
  • Earlier MCA filings, acknowledgements, pending queries and any dispute or court order affecting the request.

This is an initial preparation list, not a universal attachment checklist. Share signed records through the agreed document-sharing route; do not include passwords, DSC PINs or OTPs in an enquiry.

Official guidance: MCA DIR-11 instruction kit. DIR-11 concerns a director's notice of resignation; it should not be described as a general removal filing. Confirm current forms and instructions before submission.

Resignation and removal need different reviews

Do not treat a director's resignation notice and a company's proposed removal as interchangeable instructions. Tell us the intended route, relevant dates, available approvals and whether the matter is disputed. We confirm the documentation and applicable company process before accepting filing work.

Clarify the DIR-11 and DIR-12 scope

DIR-11 is the director's resignation notice to the Registrar. Review that requirement separately from the company's DIR-12 work; selecting a package does not mean both forms apply to every case. Align the dates and supporting records before submission, and retain acknowledgements for follow-up.

The listed packages cover agreed drafting and filing support for one director. They do not promise a contested-removal resolution, litigation representation, release from past responsibilities or an immediate MCA record update. Any additional work needs a written scope.

For connected requirements, see appointment of a replacement director, digital signature support and company annual filing.
