GST RETURN PREPARATION

Records to prepare for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

Start with the GSTIN, filing scheme and periods you need covered. Return preparation uses period-wise transactions and reconciliation records; registration documents alone do not establish filing readiness.

  • GSTIN, monthly or quarterly filing scheme, return periods and pending filings.
  • Sales register, invoices, credit/debit notes and relevant e-invoice or e-way bill records.
  • Purchase register, GSTR-2B and details of input-tax-credit differences, reversals or reverse-charge items for review.
  • Previous returns, acknowledgements, tax-payment records and unresolved mismatches or notices.
  • Authorised signatory details, records owner and approval of the proposed filing figures.

Identify missing records before work begins. If the books need preparation, discuss accounting and bookkeeping support separately. Use the compliance handover checklist to assign document owners and record unresolved items.

Preparation guidance checked on 16 November 2026 against the GST Portal's GSTR-1 guidance and GSTR-3B guidance. This is an engagement checklist, not a determination of tax-credit eligibility.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

GST return-filing questions

What are GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

GSTR-1 reports outward-supply details. GSTR-3B is a summary return covering supplies, eligible input tax credit and tax liability. The applicable forms and filing frequency depend on the taxpayer.

Does no sales automatically mean a NIL GSTR-3B?

No. Purchases, input-tax-credit claims or reversals, reverse-charge transactions and outstanding liabilities can affect NIL filing. Review the period records and the portal conditions before choosing the one-return package.

Do the annual packages include QRMP or annual GST returns?

No. The displayed annual packages cover monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B within the stated entry limits. QRMP, composition returns, GSTR-9/GSTR-9C, past-period corrections and notices need a separate agreed scope. A 12-month service package is not an annual statutory return.

Who is GST return-filing support for?

The service supports registered businesses that need help preparing and reconciling return data. Share your GSTIN, filing scheme, relevant periods and any pending returns for a scope review.

Why reconcile records before filing GST returns?

Checking sales records, purchase records and portal information helps identify omissions and mismatches before filing. A reconciliation also helps separate a data discrepancy from a question about tax-credit eligibility.

Where are GST returns filed?

Returns are filed through the official GST Portal. Keep the filing acknowledgement and reconcile the submitted figures with your business records.

When are my GST returns due?

The due date depends on the return, filing scheme and tax period, including any applicable extension. Check the current portal guidance for your registration rather than using one date for every taxpayer.

How should I prepare for GST return filing?

Bring sales and purchase records, credit or debit notes, previous return details and relevant tax-payment information. Tell us about missing records or periods without transactions so the filing position can be checked.
