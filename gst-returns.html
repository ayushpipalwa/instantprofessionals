Overview

Regular and timely filing of GST returns (GSTR‑1, GSTR‑3B, GSTR‑9, etc.) is critical to avoid interest, penalties, and input credit issues. We handle monthly/quarterly/annual return compliance.

Documents Required

  • Sales and purchase registers
  • GST invoices issued and received
  • GST PMT‑09 / cash ledger details (if applicable)
  • Previous returns (for reconciliation)

Process

  1. Reconciliation of sales/purchase data with GSTR‑2A/2B
  2. Computation of tax liability and ITC
  3. Preparation and e‑filing of applicable returns
  4. Payment of tax (if any) and intimation of filing

Fees & Timeline

Fees depend on the number of invoices, turnover, and frequency. Returns are typically filed within 1–3 working days from receipt of complete data.
