WHO IT SUITS

Who an LLP suits

An LLP under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 combines a separate legal entity with a partnership-style agreement. It is not a company and it does not issue shares.

Two or more partners

You need at least two partners and at least two designated partners. There is no statutory minimum contribution; the agreement states what each partner contributes.

Professional practices

Firms that want limited liability without company meetings and share capital often use an LLP. The agreement records profit sharing, duties and exit.

A different vehicle

Two promoters who want shares and equity investment usually need a private limited company. One Indian citizen acting alone should consider an OPC.

DOCUMENTS

Documents required

The final list depends on whether the promoters are Indian residents, NRIs or foreign nationals, and on the registered-office arrangement. Names and identity numbers must match the DSC.

  • PAN, photograph and address proof of each partner and designated partner. A foreign national uses a passport.
  • Proposed LLP names, a description of the activity, and the contribution and profit-sharing ratio.
  • DSC for the partners who will sign. DPIN or DIN for up to two designated partners can be applied for through FiLLiP if they do not already have one.
  • Registered-office proof: an ownership document, or a rent or lease agreement with a no-objection letter from the owner, plus a recent utility bill.
  • The agreed commercial terms for the LLP agreement: contributions, profit share, management rights and what happens if a partner leaves. The agreement itself is filed after incorporation.

Authoritative portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Portal instructions prevail over this overview.

PROCESS

Step-by-step process

  1. Confirm that an LLP fits. Check the number of partners, whether one designated partner is resident in India, and whether a company structure would serve the activity better.
  2. Reserve the name. Apply through RUN-LLP, or with the incorporation form where the current MCA flow allows it. Confirm that the reservation is still valid before filing FiLLiP.
  3. File FiLLiP. Prepare the incorporation form with partner details, contributions and the registered office, and sign with DSC.
  4. Collect the certificate. Track the SRN, answer any resubmission, and obtain the certificate of incorporation together with PAN and TAN.
  5. File the LLP agreement. Execute the agreement and file it in Form 3 within 30 days of incorporation. Stamp duty on the agreement depends on the state.
  6. Register only what the activity needs. GST, FSSAI, shops and establishment, or professional-tax registration is a separate decision. Incorporation does not create those registrations.
TIMELINE

Indicative timeline

These are working estimates after a complete document set. MCA processing, resubmission and DSC verification are outside a fixed promise.

  • DSC: commonly one or two working days after identity verification.
  • Name reservation: often a few working days if RUN-LLP does not ask for a change.
  • Certificate of incorporation: often about 7 to 15 working days after a complete FiLLiP filing.
  • Form 3: within 30 days of the incorporation date. Missing that window needs a separate regularisation, which is not part of the incorporation filing.

FAQ

Common questions

What is FiLLiP?

FiLLiP is the MCA form used to incorporate an LLP. It records the partners, designated partners, contribution and registered office. The LLP agreement is not part of FiLLiP; it is filed afterwards in Form 3.

How many partners does an LLP need?

At least two partners, of whom at least two are designated partners. Section 7 of the LLP Act, 2008 requires at least one designated partner to be resident in India, meaning a stay of at least 182 days during the immediately preceding one year.

Is there a minimum capital?

The LLP Act does not set a minimum contribution. The agreement must still state each partner’s contribution. Stamp duty, if any, follows the state law and the agreement value.

When is the LLP agreement filed?

Form 3 is filed within 30 days of incorporation. The agreement should be stamped as the state requires before it is uploaded.

Can a foreign national be a partner?

Yes, with a passport and DSC in that name, provided one designated partner is resident in India. Investment from outside India can need a separate FEMA check, which we scope before filing.
