Start with the situation

Useful help when the answer is not obvious.

Regulatory work often overlaps. A filing may depend on accounts; a funding request may depend on forecasts; an entity change may trigger tax and payroll actions.

“I do not know which registration applies.”

We first review the activity, constitution, place of supply, workforce and commercial plan before recommending a route.

“Several filings are pending.”

We map the defaults, dependencies and statutory exposure, then sequence the corrective work.

“A lender needs projections or a CMA.”

We define the reporting period, assumptions, information gaps and lender-specific output before preparation.

“A notice or objection has arrived.”

We verify the communication, identify the response deadline and confirm the evidence required before drafting.

  • Historical financial review and normalisation
  • Revenue, cost, working-capital and funding assumptions

About Instant Professionals

Connected judgement across compliance, tax and business decisions.

Instant Professionals is a multidisciplinary platform managed by experienced Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, Cost and Management Accountants and legal professionals. Since 2018, the practice has supported startups, MSMEs, professionals and established organisations across India.

The model is intentionally coordinated: the service is defined from the client’s situation, while the work is allocated to the relevant professional discipline. This reduces hand-offs and makes dependencies easier to see.
