LLP CLOSURE RECORDS

Prepare for an LLP strike-off review

Establish the LLP's business history and current financial position before preparing a closure application. No transactions since incorporation does not by itself establish eligibility.

  • LLPIN, incorporation certificate, current MCA status, LLP agreement and amendments.
  • Business commencement or cessation details, transaction history and latest accounts.
  • Assets, liabilities, bank-account records, charges and settlement evidence.
  • Partner details and consent, designated-partner DIN/DPIN and DSC/KYC status.
  • Form 8/Form 11 and tax filing history, GST records, pending applications, notices and disputes.

The final evidence depends on the LLP and application stage. Share records through the agreed document-sharing route; do not include passwords, DSC PINs or OTPs in an enquiry.

Official portal: Ministry of Corporate Affairs. LLP Form 24 is the strike-off application. Review current eligibility, partner-consent requirements and restrictions before filing.

Check eligibility and pending matters first

This service concerns strike-off of an eligible inactive LLP. It is distinct from liquidation or insolvency work. Package wording about winding up describes closure support here and should not be read as including those separate legal routes.

We review the inactivity history, consent of all partners, accounts, filings and pending matters before confirming the route. Open charges, partner disputes or pending applications can affect filing eligibility. Buying a package does not guarantee acceptance or removal of the LLP's name.

Compare the listed closure packages

The ₹10,200 Focused package retains the listed accounts, indemnity-bond, affidavit and document preparation for an LLP with no transactions since incorporation. The ₹11,200 Plus package also lists two-person DIR-3 KYC support; for an LLP, the relevant individuals are its designated partners, subject to applicability.

The ₹22,800 Complete package lists one financial year of Form 8/Form 11 work, a nil ITR, two DSC applications, GST cancellation and GSTR-10 work alongside the listed closure documentation. Actual records determine whether nil-return treatment and each filing are appropriate; additional years or corrections require a confirmed scope.

The written quote should specify Form 24 submission, certifications and follow-up included. Government fees, taxes, stamp duty and third-party costs remain additional unless expressly included. An application acknowledgement is not confirmation of completed strike-off; check the official outcome and remaining obligations.
