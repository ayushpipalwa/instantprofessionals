## Resolve open issues before handover
For every unresolved question, identify the missing information, the person who can resolve it, the effect on the deliverable and the next review date. Mark assumptions explicitly. Do not silently treat an unavailable record as a clean result.

## Close the handover
The receiving professional should confirm the scope, records received, unresolved items and next action. Record completion only when the agreed evidence is available. An internal status of complete does not by itself establish statutory compliance or government acceptance.

## Optional service-specific preparation
- ITR: agree the taxpayer, period, income categories and reconciliation scope with the adviser.
- Company annual filing: agree the entity, financial period, relevant approvals and records to review.
- Trademark: agree the applicant, mark representation, intended goods/services and search or filing scope.

These prompts identify the brief; they do not determine form selection, eligibility, deadlines or legal conclusions.
