What to prepare for a virtual CFO review

  • Entity structure, reporting users, internal finance responsibilities and the decisions needing support.
  • Recent financial statements, management reports and the status of the monthly close.
  • Bank balances, borrowing schedules and customer and supplier ageing reports.
  • Existing budgets, cash-flow forecasts and the assumptions behind revenue and costs.
  • Reporting deadlines, current systems, unresolved reconciliations and priority finance risks.

Use the compliance handover checklist to identify record owners and gaps. Agree a secure method for sharing financial records; do not send passwords or one-time codes through an enquiry.

Where needed, scope accounting and bookkeeping separately to improve underlying records, or discuss CMA and project reports for a defined lender or project requirement. These are not automatic additions to every monthly package.

Common questions

Defining the relationship.

Is this a full-time CFO replacement?

No fixed model is assumed. The responsibility, availability and cadence are defined for the agreed business needs and may complement internal finance staff.

Do you also maintain the books?

Bookkeeping may be included or coordinated as a separate workstream. Oversight is only useful when the underlying records are sufficiently reliable.

Can you help prepare for lender or investor discussions?

Yes, within an agreed scope covering records, projections, presentation and queries. Decisions by lenders or investors remain outside professional control.

How is the fee decided?

By reporting frequency, number of entities, quality of records, management access, team coordination and the workstreams included.
