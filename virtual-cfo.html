Common questions

Defining the relationship.

Is this a full-time CFO replacement?

No fixed model is assumed. The responsibility, availability and cadence are defined for the agreed business needs and may complement internal finance staff.

Do you also maintain the books?

Bookkeeping may be included or coordinated as a separate workstream. Oversight is only useful when the underlying records are sufficiently reliable.

Can you help prepare for lender or investor discussions?

Yes, within an agreed scope covering records, projections, presentation and queries. Decisions by lenders or investors remain outside professional control.

How is the fee decided?

By reporting frequency, number of entities, quality of records, management access, team coordination and the workstreams included.
