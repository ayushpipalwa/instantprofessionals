CORRECTION PREPARATION

Records to prepare for TDS return correction

Start with the statement already filed and the specific discrepancy. Confirm the deductor, filing period, form and processing status before deciding the correction route.

  • Deductor TAN, filing year, quarter and form, with authorised contact details.
  • Original statement acknowledgement or token number, earlier correction references and current processing status.
  • Latest consolidated statement file where required for the correction route, plus the justification report or default details where available.
  • Challans, deductee PANs and payment/deduction workings, with the affected entries and proposed changes identified.
  • Any notice, response deadline and earlier correspondence, plus certificate records if certificate work is requested.

A consolidated file represents processed statement data; a justification report explains identified defaults. They serve different purposes. Confirm the current file and portal requirements for the relevant period; do not assume every change can use the same route.

Use the compliance handover checklist to identify who holds earlier filings and supporting records. Describe the issue in your enquiry; do not send portal passwords or OTPs.

Preparation guidance checked on 16 November 2026. See the Income Tax Department's correction-statement guidance and justification-report guidance. Applicable portal instructions and law prevail.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin

What determines the final scope?

The applicant type, facts, available records, pending defaults, notices and the required filing or deliverable determine the final scope.

How long will the process take?

We confirm an expected turnaround after document review. Government or portal processing time remains outside professional control.

Are government charges included?

Only when expressly stated in the written quotation. Government fees, taxes, stamp duty and third-party charges are otherwise additional.

From discrepancy review to correction follow-up

  1. Review the filed statement, processing status and discrepancy to confirm the permitted correction route and scope.
  2. Reconcile affected records against challans, deductee details and the latest available statement data.
  3. Prepare the changes, obtain approval of the figures and validate or submit through the applicable route.
  4. Retain the correction acknowledgement and check processing results; confirm any further response or certificate work required.

Submitting a correction does not guarantee that every default or notice is resolved. Any additional tax, interest, fee or further correction needs separate review. Turnaround depends on records, access and portal processing.

Correction or original TDS filing?

This service addresses an existing statement. If the return has not yet been filed, see TDS return filing and annual packages. For missing underlying records, discuss bookkeeping support. A correction package is not an annual filing subscription or a blanket engagement for every notice.
