# Legal content maintenance

Reviewed baseline: 12 September 2026

## Scope

This control covers the active Instant Professionals service pages in the searchable service directory. The shared legal-currency layer maps each page to an official primary-source family and displays the review date, governing framework, verification note and authority link.

## Source hierarchy

1. Enacted Act and amendment Act
2. Notified rules, regulations and schemes
3. Gazette notifications and statutory circulars
4. Official portal forms, utilities and advisories
5. Department FAQs and guidance
6. Secondary commentary only for issue spotting; never as the controlling source

## Review matrix

| Service family | Primary authorities | Core framework |
|---|---|---|
| Income Tax & TDS | Income Tax Department, CBDT, India Budget, e-Gazette | Income-tax Act 2025/Rules 2026 for TY 2026–27 onward; 1961 Act/1962 Rules for earlier periods and transitions |
| GST & indirect tax | CBIC, GST Council, GST portal, e-Gazette | CGST/IGST and applicable State/UT GST Acts, rules, notifications and circulars |
| Corporate & secretarial | MCA, e-Gazette | Companies Act 2013, LLP Act 2008 and applicable rules/forms |
| Workforce compliance | EPFO, ESIC, Ministry of Labour, e-Gazette | EPF & MP Act/schemes; ESI Act/regulations; applicable notified changes |
| Intellectual property | IP India, Copyright Office, DPIIT, e-Gazette | Trade Marks, Copyright and Patents Acts and Rules |
| Import & export | DGFT, CBIC Customs, e-Gazette | FTDR Act, FTP 2023, HBP, notifications and product restrictions |
| Business registrations | Ministry of MSME, Income Tax Department, CCA and relevant government portals | Registration-specific Central, State and local law |
| Business growth | India Code and relevant regulator | Contract, consumer, advertising, IP, data, tax and sector rules as scoped |

## Mandatory review points

- Effective date and transition provision
- Section, rule and form renumbering
- Threshold, rate, fee and limitation changes
- Portal utility or filing-mode changes
- Authority, jurisdiction and approval route
- Document checklist and evidence requirements
- Due date and processing-time language
- Pricing text that assumes a government fee
- Calculator inputs, presets, formula and disclaimer
- Links to official sources
- Page reviewed date and sitemap lastmod

A confirmed change is implemented through a focused pull request. No unattended workflow may publish a legal interpretation or calculator-rate change directly to `gh-pages`.
