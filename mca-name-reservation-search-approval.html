COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reserve a name

Does this guarantee MCA name approval?

No. Name approval or reservation is the decision of the MCA/Registrar. Our work is a professional pre-filing review and filing-support service intended to reduce avoidable risks and make the application better prepared.

Is a company-name search the same as a trademark search?

No. MCA/company-name and trademark records serve different purposes. We screen both relevant areas at a practical level, but trademark registration, clearance opinions, opposition work and litigation are separate specialist scopes unless expressly agreed.

Can this be used for a new company or LLP?

Yes, subject to the entity type and current MCA process. We confirm whether the proposed matter concerns a new incorporation, an LLP, or a change of name for an existing entity before finalising the scope.

Why does the service start at ₹25,000?

The fee reflects the extended research, name-rule analysis, written shortlist work and coordinated filing support required for a meaningful pre-filing assessment. Complex names, regulated words, multiple activities, extensive searches or additional responses may require a separate written quotation.
