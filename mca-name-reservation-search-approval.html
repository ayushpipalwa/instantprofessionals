SERVICE QUESTIONS

MCA name reservation questions

What does reserving a business name achieve?

Name reservation is one step in the relevant MCA process. It does not by itself incorporate the entity or provide trademark registration; each process has its own requirements.

Who is the name-reservation review for?

Founders planning a company or LLP, and businesses considering a name change, can request a review. Share the proposed names, intended activity and entity type before the application route is selected.

Where is an MCA name-reservation application submitted?

The applicable application is submitted through the official MCA portal. Our service supports screening and preparation; approval remains with the competent authority.

When should I arrange a company-name search?

Arrange screening before committing to incorporation documents or a brand launch. If a name has already been approved, check the approval conditions and validity before relying on it.

Official guidance: MCA company and LLP services. General service information checked on 28 September 2026. The applicable rules and portal requirements depend on your circumstances and prevail over this overview.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reserve a name

Does this guarantee MCA name approval?

No. Name approval or reservation is the decision of the MCA/Registrar. Our work is a professional pre-filing review and filing-support service intended to reduce avoidable risks and make the application better prepared.

Is a company-name search the same as a trademark search?

No. MCA/company-name and trademark records serve different purposes. We screen both relevant areas at a practical level, but trademark registration, clearance opinions, opposition work and litigation are separate specialist scopes unless expressly agreed.

Can this be used for a new company or LLP?

Yes, subject to the entity type and current MCA process. We confirm whether the proposed matter concerns a new incorporation, an LLP, or a change of name for an existing entity before finalising the scope.
